## Multiple Choice

1. Which description best matches physical hydrology?
   - A. Classification of biological species living near water
   - B. Study of water occurrence, movement, and physical properties on and within Earth and its atmosphere
   - C. Study of ocean chemistry alone
   - D. Design of municipal pipes without environmental measurements

2. Which item belongs to the cryosphere?
   - A. An aquifer
   - B. A river
   - C. A glacier
   - D. A cloud containing liquid droplets

3. Approximately what fraction of Earth's total water is in the oceans?
   - A. $37\%$
   - B. $57\%$
   - C. $77\%$
   - D. $97\%$

4. Which reservoir contains the larger volume of Earth's liquid freshwater?
   - A. Groundwater
   - B. Rivers
   - C. Swamps
   - D. Lakes

5. Which unit is most appropriate for river discharge?
   - A. $\mathrm{mm}$
   - B. $\mathrm{km^2}$
   - C. $\mathrm{m^3/s}$
   - D. $\mathrm{kg/m^3}$

6. Which additional measurement is required to convert a precipitation depth to a water volume?
   - A. Air temperature
   - B. Drainage area
   - C. Wind direction
   - D. Water density

7. A lake receives $I$ units of water and releases $O$ units during a study period. Which expression gives storage change?
   - A. $\Delta S=O-I$
   - B. $\Delta S=I+O$
   - C. $\Delta S=I-O$
   - D. $\Delta S=IO$

8. What does the peak of a storm hydrograph represent?
   - A. The lowest measured precipitation
   - B. The highest discharge response
   - C. The total watershed area
   - D. The groundwater divide

9. Which feature defines the outer surface boundary of a watershed?
   - A. A topographic divide
   - B. A treatment plant
   - C. A rain gauge
   - D. A lake's average depth

10. What is a tributary?
    - A. A ridge separating two basins
    - B. A smaller watercourse entering a larger stream or river
    - C. A point where all groundwater evaporates
    - D. A reservoir with no outlet

11. In the conceptual watershed, why may $Q_{in}$ be omitted?
    - A. Surface water cannot move through watersheds.
    - B. The complete headwater boundary shows no upstream channel entering the system.
    - C. Precipitation always replaces surface inflow.
    - D. $Q$ is a groundwater term.

12. Which condition defines steady state?
    - A. Inputs are zero.
    - B. Outputs are zero.
    - C. Inputs equal outputs and $\Delta S=0$.
    - D. Storage is empty.

13. A full rain barrel receives $30\ \mathrm{L/h}$ from the eaves and releases $30\ \mathrm{L/h}$ through overflow. What is its condition?
    - A. Storage is increasing.
    - B. Storage is decreasing.
    - C. It is at steady state.
    - D. It has no water movement.

14. Which Great Lake has the largest volume in the course table?
    - A. Erie
    - B. Huron
    - C. Ontario
    - D. Superior

15. Why is lake-effect snow concentrated east and southeast of the Great Lakes?
    - A. Prevailing winds generally carry moisture from west to east across warmer open water.
    - B. Groundwater always flows east.
    - C. Eastern shores are all at sea level.
    - D. Evaporation occurs only near eastern shores.

16. Where does the Grand River ultimately discharge?
    - A. Lake Ontario
    - B. Lake Erie
    - C. Lake Huron
    - D. Lake Superior

## Calculations and Applications

35. A reservoir receives $120{,}000\ \mathrm{m^3}$ and releases $95{,}000\ \mathrm{m^3}$ during one month. Calculate $\Delta S$ and state whether storage increased or decreased.

36. A watershed has $P=70\ \mathrm{mm}$, $G_{in}=8\ \mathrm{mm}$, $Q_{in}=0$, $ET=35\ \mathrm{mm}$, $G_{out}=5\ \mathrm{mm}$, and $Q_{out}=28\ \mathrm{mm}$. Calculate $\Delta S$.

37. A rain barrel receives $I_E=60\ \mathrm{L}$ and $I_R=4\ \mathrm{L}$. It releases $O_O=14\ \mathrm{L}$ and $O_S=10\ \mathrm{L}$. Calculate the storage change.

38. Rainfall depth is $12\ \mathrm{mm}$ over an area of $2\ \mathrm{km^2}$. Calculate the rainfall volume in cubic metres.

39. A full rain barrel has eaves inflow of $18\ \mathrm{L/min}$, direct rainfall input of $1\ \mathrm{L/min}$, no spigot flow, and overflow of $19\ \mathrm{L/min}$. Verify whether it is at steady state.

40. The Grand River's average flow is $55\ \mathrm{m^3/s}$. Estimate the volume passing a point during one hour.
