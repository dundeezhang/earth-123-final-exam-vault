## Highest-Yield Exam Points

1. Weekly modules organize time management, an introduction, learning outcomes, content, activities and assignments, key terms, and optional resources.
2. The Course Schedule is the main source for course timing, activities, assignments, and due dates.
3. Quizzes, assignments, and discussion posts are learning components with qualitative and quantitative work.
4. Optional resources provide examples and context, but the Module 1 page states that they are excluded from course assessments.
5. Scientific inquiry moves from an observation or question to a testable explanation, evidence collection, analysis, and a conclusion that may revise the explanation.
6. A useful hypothesis must generate a prediction that evidence can test.
7. Variables and controls help isolate cause-and-effect relationships.
8. Scientific conclusions are provisional because new evidence can support, modify, or reject an explanation.
9. The Module 1 film focuses on Canadian waterways, reserves, and networks and treats water as both a benefit and a challenge.
10. Course structure and scientific method serve different purposes: one organizes learning, while the other organizes investigation.
