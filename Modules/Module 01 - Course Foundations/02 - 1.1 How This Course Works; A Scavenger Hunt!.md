# 1.1 How This Course Works; A Scavenger Hunt!

Up: [[00 - EARTH 123 Final Exam Home|Home]]

Module: [[Modules/Module 01 - Course Foundations/00 - Module 01 Overview|Overview]] | Definitions: [[Modules/Module 01 - Course Foundations/01 - Main Definitions|Definitions]]

## Exam Focus

Know where course information is organized, the standard weekly-module structure, the role of assessments, and the difference between required and optional material.

## Core Notes

### Accessing course material

- The course Content area provides access to course files and content.
- The Table of Contents lists the course outline, activities and assignments, and content modules.
- The Course Schedule gives an organized view of course content, assessments, and due dates.

### Weekly module structure

Each weekly module follows a recurring structure:

1. Time management
2. Introduction
3. Learning outcomes
4. Module content
5. Activities and assignments
6. Key terms
7. Optional readings and resources

Weekly modules introduce physical-hydrology fundamentals and connect hydrology to population distribution, supply and demand, land-use management, and natural hazards.

### Assessments

- The course uses short weekly quizzes, assignments, and discussion posts.
- Assessment work includes qualitative and quantitative interpretation of hydrologic data.
- These activities are learning components within the course framework, rather than separate from the content.

### Scavenger-hunt purpose

The scavenger hunt asks students to locate six kinds of course information:

1. Reading Week timing in the Course Schedule.
2. Number of quizzes in Activities and Assessments.
3. Assignment percentages in the Grade Breakdown.
4. The technical-support contact in Contact Information.
5. Estimated completion time on the Week 2 page.
6. Submission instructions in the introductory discussion.

The source page asks students to find these answers elsewhere. It does not provide the answers itself.

### Optional resources

- Weekly optional readings, websites, and videos provide examples, animations, images, and stories.
- The source states that these optional links are excluded from course assessments.
- They may still support discussion posts and conceptual understanding.

## Common Confusions

- **Course Schedule vs. weekly module:** the Course Schedule organizes timing across the course; a weekly module contains one week's learning sequence.
- **Assessment vs. optional resource:** quizzes, assignments, and discussions are assessed learning components; optional resources are enrichment.
- **Scavenger-hunt prompt vs. answer:** 1.1 records where to find each answer, not the current answer itself.
