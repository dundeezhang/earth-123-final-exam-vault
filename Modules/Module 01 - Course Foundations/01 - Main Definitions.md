## Commonly Confused Terms

- **Hypothesis vs. prediction:** a hypothesis is the proposed explanation; a prediction is the expected observation if that explanation is correct.
- **Independent vs. dependent variable:** the independent variable is the proposed influence; the dependent variable is the measured response.
- **Observation vs. conclusion:** an observation begins or informs an investigation; a conclusion interprets the collected evidence.
- **Qualitative vs. quantitative:** qualitative work explains characteristics and relationships; quantitative work uses numbers and calculations.
- **Required content vs. optional resource:** required module content supports assessment; 1.1 states that optional resources are excluded from assessments.
- **Water reserve vs. waterway:** a reserve emphasizes storage; a waterway emphasizes connection or movement.
