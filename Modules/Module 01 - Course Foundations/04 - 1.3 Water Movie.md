## Exam Focus

Know the film's stated scope: Canadian waterways, water reserves, water networks, and water's historical and present role as both a benefit and a challenge.

## Core Notes

- Module 1 assigns the National Film Board of Canada film *Water: Reserves and Networks*.
- The course page states that Canada possesses the greatest supply of freshwater and salt water on the planet.
- The film examines Canadian waterways, reserves, and networks.
- It considers water's role in the past and present.
- Water is framed as precious and as both a boon and a challenge.

### Hydrologic interpretation

- **Storage:** reserves hold water in lakes, rivers, groundwater, snow, ice, and managed systems.
- **Connection:** networks link water stores through natural flows and human infrastructure.
- **Use:** water supports communities, transportation, ecosystems, industry, and resource development.
- **Risk:** the same water system can create scarcity, contamination, flooding, access, and management challenges.

The four points above are an exam-oriented interpretation of the film topic. The saved page provides the film description and reference, while the film itself remains external.
