# 12.3 The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC) and the Future of Climate Change Research

Up: [[Modules/Module 12 - Climate Change and Hydrology/00 - Module 12 Overview|Module 12 Overview]]

## Exam Focus

Know why the IPCC exists, its four conclusions, its broad projections, and the responses the lesson asks individuals and organizations to make.

## Core Notes

### IPCC Role

- **IPCC** stands for **Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change**.
- It was established by the United Nations in **1988**.
- The saved course page states that it has **195 member countries**.
- It leads and organizes thousands of scientists and researchers and is diverse enough to include many perspectives rather than being dominated by one group.
- It facilitates rapid sharing of research, ideas, theories, data, and knowledge among scientists and with the wider population and media.

### Principal Conclusions

The course lists four central IPCC conclusions:

1. Warming of the atmosphere, ocean, and cryosphere is unequivocal and accelerating.
2. There is very high confidence that the net effect of human activities since approximately 1750 has been warming.
3. Anthropogenic warming has measurably affected physical and biological systems.
4. Continued emissions at or above current rates will cause further warming and twenty-first-century changes likely larger than those observed during the twentieth century.

### Broad Projections

- Higher global average temperatures.
- Fewer cold and frost days.
- More intense precipitation events.
- Higher risk of long-duration drought.
- Increased hurricane intensity.
- Short-term records will remain noisy and may temporarily move against the long-term trend.

### Responses

- Limiting personal waste and energy use is an important first step, but personal action alone is insufficient.
- Better environmental stewardship, new technology, and new energy resources are additional approaches named by the lesson.
- The course asks people to use their **voice** and **wallet** to influence how large organizations use resources and energy and generate waste.
- Climate change requires people to work together as a global team.

## Common Confusions

- The IPCC coordinates scientists and knowledge sharing; it is not one research group or country.
- Short-term fluctuations do not invalidate long-term projections.
- Higher hurricane **intensity** is different from a demonstrated historical rise in total hurricane **count**.
- Individual choices are a first step, not the only solution.
